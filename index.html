    02 — Background

    About

    I’m Jun, a student who writes software around games and sports data. I like problems with a clear score: a hand to play, a category to fill, a line to price.

    Most of that work is in Rust and Python. got-poker is a GTO engine for preflop and postflop decisions. prizepicks-scraper is a CS2 projection pipeline. yahtzee is a desktop dice game with a live scorecard. This site is the other current project — a single page with an ASCII card-suit hero and the work listed above.

    I want to keep building things that make a decision clearer: solvers, pipelines, small interfaces. LinkedIn is the right way to reach me about any of it.
